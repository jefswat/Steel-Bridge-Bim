# Agent review - Northaven Trail tied arch (span 10) curbs

Model: `samples/plans/Northaven NHT.sbm`, bridge "Arch span 10" (curbs `Curb LT` / `Curb RT` from `samples/plans/arch-nht.csx` lines 465-466, rebar lines 437-449).
Generator: `src/SBM.Core/Arch/ArchGenerator.cs` `BuildCurbs` (v7 branch), bars `src/SBM.Core/Arch/ArchGeneratorDeck.cs` lines 225-296.
Plans: `C:\SBMData\0047-07-228 NHT 100% Plans_09-04-2020_comments_Reduced.pdf`, p.83 (general notes), p.90 (arch typical section), p.124 (knuckle),
p.145 (floorbeam), p.151 (deck plan), p.152 (deck section), p.154 (bar bends + bill). The sheets were read from PyMuPDF crops (`work/p*.png`).

How it was measured: debug CLI `sbm run <script> "Northaven NHT.sbm" --no-pipeline` with three read-only scripts in `work/` (`curbs.csx`,
`curbs2.csx`, `curbs3.csx`; outputs `*-out.txt`). They read the generated curb mesh (`ArchModel.Curbs`), the S1 / D4 bar placements and marks,
the steel parts and the railing runs: every curb vertex as station / offset / height above the finished grade
(`Deck.FinishedElevation`), ray-parity inside tests and point-to-triangle distances for the bars against the concrete and the steel, horizontal and
vertical rays at the curb ends against the knuckle end webs (`KNUCKLE-*-EW`), and railing post bases against the curb top. Nothing was saved.

## Summary

The curb position, size, top height, S1 / D4 counts, sizes, covers and the bill totals match the plans. The open points are:
- the plans chamfer the top corners 3/4" (p.83 note 3, drawn on p.90 / p.152); the model curb is a plain rectangle;
- the S1 hooks of Curb RT are at the trail-side top corner, the plan puts them at the outer (tie) corner on both curbs;
- the D4 bottom bars and the S1 legs pass through every floorbeam web, and nothing reports it. p.145 holes the webs only for D2. This is a conflict between p.152 and p.145;
- at the obtuse knuckle corners (Curb LT east, Curb RT west) the last 1'-9" of curb has no S1 / D4 at all;
- the arch curbs are not elements of their own. They are a second body of the deck IfcSlab ("deck slab" in the database), while the approach curbs are IfcKerb / "curb".

Count by severity: wrong 1, missing 2, plan conflict 2, unclear 5, OK 14 (24 rows).
