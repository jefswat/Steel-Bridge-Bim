# PTC Delaware River Bridge - agent review of the curbs and parapets

Agent review (2026-10-30) of the concrete curb + parapet line items ("Left / Right curb + parapet"), the abutment wing parapets ("Left / Right abutment parapet (W) / (E)") and the aluminum parapet railing on them. They are built by `samples/plans/deck-ptc.csx` on all seven bridges of `samples/plans/PTC Delaware River Bridge.sbm`.

How it was done:
- The model was regenerated once with the debug CLI (`qc/ptc/curbs/work/dump.csx`, output `work/dump.txt`). The script dumped each line item's definition, its mesh volume, the grade at the gutter line and outer face, the railing runs and posts, and tested every steel part vertex against the curb / parapet section.
- The plan sheets were read from crops in `work/*.png`: p.107 Sections A / B / C and the elevations, p.108 abutment elevations, parapet expansion joints and abutment parapet details, p.91 dam end section, p.42 quantities.
- Nothing in the model, scripts or data was changed.

## Summary

- **Matching the plan:**
  - gutter line at 38'-3 (76'-6 curb to curb);
  - 2'-10 section (82'-2 overall);
  - 9 in curb face, 2'-0 curb, 10 in x 1'-9 wall;
  - section area (527.5 in2 in the model vs about 529 in2 drawn);
  - railing height and offset;
  - abutment parapet lengths 67'-9 / 34'-9;
  - railing LF vs p.42 item 13;
  - no steel inside the curb or parapet on any bridge;
  - Class A concrete within 1.1 % of p.42 item 11 overall.
- **Wrong:**
  - The curb top rise is printed as 1/2 in. The model uses 1/4 in, so the parapet top is 1/4 in low.
  - The model curb face is vertical. The plans show it battered 1 in.
  - The slab runs out under the whole wall (to 2'-10). The plans end it 1'-11 from the gutter line, with the wall overhanging the slab end by 11 in. That is about 287 CY of slab that is not on the plans.
  - The abutment railing runs get one extra post per side at each wing end.
- **Missing:**
  - Drainage openings: 2'-2 long at 4'-2 centres, 4 1/4 in high, with a 5 1/2 in slot. About 212 CY if every span has them.
  - Breaks of the curb, parapet and railing at the internal dams (piers 1-9, 28-30, W22).
  - Steel parapet / bulkhead ends at the finger dams.
  - Parapet panel joints.
  - The element status flag (it never reaches IFC / database).
- **Flag text:** the curb flag names pages and a reason, but its reason is partly inaccurate. Opening size and radii are printed, and its basis says "+1/4 in".

Counts: 11 OK, 5 wrong, 5 missing, 3 unclear, 1 plan conflict (25 rows).
