## Method
- Rivets were taken from `out/rivets_<tag>.csv`: each shank was traced from its start point along its axis over its grip, and the parts it passes through were listed using the `tris_<tag>.csv` triangles.
  - A member end counts as connected when its rivets pass through member + plate (+ fill).
  - A plate counts as fastened when its edge angle has rivets through both the plate and the stack (gusset, chord plies, splice plates).
  - Air gaps were measured inside every grip.
  - Interference: sample points of each bracing part were tested for being inside another closed part. Penetration above 1/16 in. was found nowhere.
- The rivet counts were compared with p.71 TOP CHORD LATERAL SYSTEM: 26 rivets each end of the lateral diagonals, 40 each end of the struts, 16 at U22.
- The images were then checked view by view.
- Scale: p.98 was checked at 2.14 pt/in on the printed 1'-2 1/2 W.P. dimension (TRUSS-DESIGN uses 2.166). p.90 was scaled at about 2.0 pt/in on its '2'-5 o.o. Web Pls.' dimension.

## Systematic patterns
- Upper lateral system U12-U22 is OK: 0 of 52 member ends inspected on S / at the apexes are unconnected. All rivet counts match p.71.
- U11 is broken: the portal, the lateral and the top plate are all disconnected. Cause: the U11 plates are generic envelope plates in the lateral plane (TRUSS-DESIGN 39 d, 'not a generator input'). They sit 10-13 in. away from the 36 in. portal box and above the chord. The generator then bridges the lateral with 10-12 in. fills and puts the top edge angle in air.
  - Suggested generic checks: a fill thicker than about 1 in., an edge angle with no stack under its leg, and a bracing member with zero rivets should each raise a CHECK warning.
- Envelopes not yet traced (minor, already flagged): the UM apex plates (p.98 half view) and U22 (p.90).
- Review tooling (`render_bracing.py`): a triangle is drawn when any one vertex is in the 60 in. band. Long lateral triangles therefore show up far outside the band; in S_U12 view B, UL-SU11-UM12 appears along the chord.
