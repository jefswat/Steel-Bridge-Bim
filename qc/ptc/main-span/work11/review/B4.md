## Systematic issues
1. **No plate is fastened to the truss or floor system anywhere in the suspended floor lateral system.** The FX plates have no floor beam rivets. The W plates
   reach neither the chord nor the bent angles. The p.99 floor beam web angles (L 4 1/2x3 1/2x1/2 N.S. / L 3 1/2x3 1/2x1/2 F.S., 2-Ls 8x8x1/2 at W14) are not
   built. The L 3 1/2x3x3/8 "edge angles" are placed 9 in. off the web and join nothing.
2. **One-sided W plates.** Each W point builds a plate only for the lateral that comes from the lower FX. The lateral to the next FX (and the K member at W21)
   has nothing. This is the same as the L11' pattern in the brief: the plate hangs off the member tip.
3. **Top flange / top plate never riveted.** Laterals are riveted on the bottom flange only, so about 40 % of the p.71 count per 14 BP 73 end (8 of 20) and 46 %
   per 2-15[ 50 end (24 of 52).
4. **Wind chord splices at W15 / W17 / W19 / W21 are missing.** The chord pieces butt at the point with 0 rivets.
5. **LQ13 / LQ13' and WM22 have no connection material at all.** The tongue beam (16 WF 88 + covers), its 14 WF 48 cross piece and the W21 tongue connection
   are not modeled. The W21 K apex should sit 2'-0 toward W21 (p.93), not on the F7 CL (minor).

## Notes
- No interference over 1/4 in. was found at these points. The K ends at WM22 stop 0.5-1 in. off the F7 web plies, and the W plates are 0.3 in. below the
  lateral flanges.
- Rivet counts read from the drawings (~34 chord rivets per W plate, ~22 field rivets into the bent angles, ~10 per FX floor beam column, ~14-16 per W33 at
  LQ13) are symbol counts on the raster. They are not printed numbers. The p.71 per-end counts (20 / 52 / 16) are printed.
- The model's wind chord orientation (web vertical, the lateral plate under the bottom flange) agrees with p.99 Section C (2-Pls 11x3/8 web splice, plates on
  the top and bottom flanges). The p.99 Section A side sketch is ambiguous, so it was not reported.
