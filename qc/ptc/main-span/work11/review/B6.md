This review was read-only. I wrote only `B6.csv`, this file and the `B6_*.png` crops.

Points (54): L12' U12' SW12' LW12' UW12' UM12' LM12' L11' U11' SW11' LM11' L10'-L0', SW10'-SW0', LW10'-LW1', LM10'-LM0'.
All dumps are S plane (`png\S_<point>p.png`, `out\tris_S_*p.csv`, `rivets_S_*p.csv`).

## Method
- **Rivets.** Each rivet shank runs from the frame origin (the bearing face under the head) to origin + grip along +Z. I sampled 9 points along it and tested them
  against every part mesh (ray parity) to get the parts each rivet grips.
- **Joint types.** A member end is counted as connected only when:
  - rivets grip the member AND its plate / fill / end angle, and
  - the plate group (the plates within 2 in. of the member's last 30 in., plus their -EA / -F siblings) has rivets into a part outside the group (gusset, chord,
    splice plate, floor beam, post, strut).

  Separately, I counted the rivets that grip only plate + angle, and the rivets that grip fill + plate with no member.
- **Gaps.** Plate-to-stack and tip-to-stack distances are point-to-triangle.
- **Interference.** Member sample points inside other parts, exact depth. None is deeper than 0.12 in. at any end in this batch.
- **Mirror check.** I ran the same analysis on the 54 unprimed dumps and compared each end with u mirrored.
- **Scratch scripts.** `anal.py`, `rivparts.py` and `bbx.py` are in the session scratchpad `b6x\`.
- **Rivet counts.** The audit's `n_rivets` are box counts. The counts here are the shank-test counts.
