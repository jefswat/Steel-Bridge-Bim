## Missing connections (major)
1. **L11 (unprimed twin of L11'): the same defect as L11'.** Both S laterals (LL-SL11-LM10 and LL-SL11-LM12) are riveted only to their 5/8 plates (10 + 10 rivets). All four edge angles L 8 x 7 1/2 x 1/2 have **0 rivets**, either to the plate or to the gusset stack, so the plates are not fastened to the truss. The lateral tips stop 37 / 48 in. from the W.P. In plan, the plates run from about 17 in. to 55 in. north of the chord line.
   - The plates do touch the edge angles, and the angles touch gusset layer 4, except S-BGL11-b-B-EA. That angle lies mostly past the gusset edge and floats 1.24 in. off it.
   - S-BGL11-b-T-EA carries 2 rivets, but they are truss joint rivets that happen to pass through its leg. They do not fasten the plate.
   - parts.csv already says "angle rivets not placed". The flag is accurate, but the connection does not exist.
   - p.80 Section L / View K draws a bent plate with an edge angle riveted to the chord. p.71 asks for 36 / 40 rivets per end; the model has 20.
   - The N truss ends are NOT SHOWN (no N image). The audit shows the same parts, so the N side is presumably mirrored.
2. **LM11 sway bottom (SW-N11 / SW-S11).** The plate pair sits on top of the L11 strut and has no rivet into it.
   - Its L 3 1/2 x 3 x 3/8 edge angles lie outside the strut width, 1.2 - 1.3 in. off the strut, and are riveted only to the plates.
   - p.80 Section A draws the plates lapping down over the strut sides, with two rows of 1 in. rivets, and the 2-Ls 3 1/2 x 3 x 3/8 at the plate TOP edge.
3. **LM12 sway bottom (SW-N12 / SW-S12).** Both diagonals end 67 - 70 in. from the CL, while the sway plates end at +-56 in. That is a 14 in. or more gap with 0 rivets.
   - The plates and edge angles also float 5.8 - 6.8 in. above the rolled L12 strut, with no angle-to-strut rivets.
   - The SW-N12 reason text is stale: it still says "p.97-100 not read".
4. **LM13 lateral apex.** The plate pair joins the two L12 - LM13 laterals only (24 + 24 rivets). It hangs 17.6 - 19.5 in. below the F8 bottom flange, and F8 is the strut at L13.
   - p.95 Section D carries the 1/2 gusset plates up to the F8 bottom flange with L 9 x 4 x 1 angles.
5. **L13 floor lateral FL-X13-15b1 (and a1 on the N side).** No plate and no rivets.
   - p.83 Section B draws a 3/4 bent gusset, 1-L 8 x 8 x 3/4 on the chord and 1-L 8 x 4 x 1 on F8.
   - This is a known open item (TRUSS-DESIGN 39 (e), truss-level flag), confirmed again here.
6. **UNSURE, major: L11 bottom strut end tees.** The strut laps the 11 5/8 in. tees only 6.8 in., with 9 rivets per web; another 18 rivets have no strut behind them. p.80 Section A draws field rivet bands about 2'-9 long along the strut, about 54 rivets in all. I scaled this on "2'-6 1/2 b. to b. Ls" (8.4 px/in. at 300 dpi). The tee orientation needs confirming on Section A / Half Section E.
