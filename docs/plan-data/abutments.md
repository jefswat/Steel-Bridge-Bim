# Abutment plan data (Iowa DOT Design 116 WB I-80, Design 316 Ramp G)

Transcribed from the plan PDFs. Values are ft-in as printed; elevations exact. "pN" = PDF page N; "DS n" = printed
"DESIGN SHEET NO. n". Items marked UNCERTAIN are best readings.

Sheet mapping (verified from title blocks):
- Design 116 (B00168): West abutment p85-88 (DS 83-86), East abutment p89-92 (DS 87-90), Abutment Aesthetic p93 (DS 91).
  For I-80, PDF = DS + 2 (printed sheet number = PDF - 1).
- Design 316 (B00169): South abutment p80-83 (DS 78-81), Aesthetic p84 (DS 82). PDF = DS + 2.
- Sheet contents: sheet 1 of each set = plan/rear elevation/anchor bolt layout; sheet 2 = piling layout, section through
  abutment (Design 116 west: p86; east: p89 has the section and p90 the piling); sheet 3 = wingwall views + sections +
  maskwall; sheet 4 = bar list, bent bar details, abutment notes, section H-H (wing footing/counterfort), quantities.
  (West: p85 plan/rear elev/section is on p86 - see below; East: p89 plan+rear elev+section, p90 piling, p91 wings, p92 bars.)

General: all three abutments are the same TYPE - pile-supported footing + vertical backwall with approach-slab paving
notch (corbel) on the back face, bearing seats (beam steps/pedestals) on the footing top in FRONT of the backwall, an
expansion device in the upper backwall (poured after the deck), full-height wingwalls that are SEPARATE concrete placements
doweled to the backwall (bars 6g4 / 6f4) and carried on counterforts on 2 wing footings each, plus a maskwall (end wall
behind the barrier). Upper backwall is poured after the expansion joint is set. Not MSE, not integral.
Title-block data: West/East stationing of the abutment CL bearing is NOT given on these sheets (the title block
"STATION 7595+67.24, 41.08' LT. I-80" is the structure reference). Bench marks: I-80 "BENCH MARK NO. 597 STA. 4298+67.763,
81.85' LT. FD IHC SE CORNER WING WESTBOUND I-80 BRIDGE OVER RR YARD ELEVATION 1012.698" (p85-93); Ramp G "BENCH MARK NO. 510
STA. 4276+77.619, 254.61' LT. FD IHC BM IN SE WING I-80/I-29 OVER I-80 E. ELEVATION 1007.367" (p80-83).
Abutment Notes (p88 I-80, p83 Ramp G, same text): standard abutment details modified to OFFSET THE FOOTING FROM THE BACKWALL
to tie bars; min clear 2" unless noted; maskwall poured before slab; keyways = beveled 2x8 (Ramp G view C-C: 2x6);
backwall portion containing the expansion-device anchorage placed AFTER deck; sealer on bridge seat and wash; beams and
masonry plates set before backwall placed; no welding of anchor bolts; barrier rails not shown in abutment details.
