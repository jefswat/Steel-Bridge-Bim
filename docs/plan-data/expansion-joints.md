# Expansion joints and deck-end details (I-80 WB Design 116, Ramp G Design 316)

Source: plan PDFs in `dist/SteelBridgeModeler/` (B00168 = I-80 WB, B00169 = Ramp G). Page tags are PDF page with the design sheet
in parentheses. Values are ft-in as printed; fractions were confirmed on raster clips. Working files: `temp/extract/joints/`.

## 1. Summary

| Item | Finding |
|---|---|
| Joint types | Every I-80 joint (both abutments; Piers 5WB, 6A, 11WB, 19G) is a MODULAR expansion device. Ramp G: MODULAR at Piers 5G, 9G, 14G; the Pier 19G joint is detailed only on the I-80 sheets (Ramp G pdf 152 says to see the I-80 bridge). Ramp G SOUTH ABUTMENT = STEEL EXTRUSION (strip seal) with neoprene gland, Iowa DOT "F-shape" standard. No finger joints anywhere. |
| Who designs the joint | Modular: the manufacturer (shop drawings; anchorage and bracket spacing by the manufacturer). Strip seal: plans give approved products, extrusion layout, anchorage plates and blockout, but extrusion and gland are vendor items. |
| Verdict | Modular: (b) envelope/blockout plus generic joint body. Strip seal: (b), nearly (a) for the anchorage plates. No joint is (a). |

## 2. I-80 WB modular joints (pdf 209 = design 202, pdf 210 = design 203)

### 2.1 Movement and blockout table (pdf 209)
Printed as "MOVEMENT & BLOCKOUT TABLE"; C and D are marked "perpendicular to joint".

| Location | Total thermal movement (in) | Temp change per 1/8" adjustment (F) | Dim "C" (in) | Dim "D" (in) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| West Abutment | 8.8 | 3.0 | 6 | 18 |
| Pier No. 5WB | 11.0 | 1.7 | 9 | 25 |
| Pier No. 6A | 9.6 | 2.0 | 9 | 25 |
| Pier No. 11WB | 12.8 | 1.6 | 9 | 25 |
| Pier No. 19G | 6.0 | 3.6 | 9 | 25 |
| East Abutment | 6.4 | 2.9 | 6 | 18 |

Notes on the sheet: dimensions marked (*) are at 50 F with 1/8" variation for the temperature in the table (increase the gap for a
temperature decrease, decrease it for an increase); "**" = dimensions set by the joint manufacturer. Movements are based on a
150 degree range and occur along a line from the assumed point of zero movement to the joint (design sheet 155).

Reading of the drawn dimension arrows (my interpretation, UNCERTAIN as to wording because the notes do not define C and D):
- C = gap between the two deck-end faces at 50 F (9" at piers, 6" at abutments). Consistent with the slab sheets (4 1/2" each side of CL pier;
  6" deck end to backwall face at abutments).
- D = blockout width on each side, from the deck-end face to the blockout edge (25" = 2'-1 at piers, 18" = 1'-6 at abutments). Consistent with
  the slab sections (2.4).

### 2.2 What is dimensioned (pdf 209-210)
- Section at pier and section at abutment: blockout is a stepped pocket with a deeper box around the support bar; slab/blockout depth
  1'-2; widths D (table). Centre beams, edge beams, support bar and support box are drawn and labelled; their sizes are NOT dimensioned.
- Joint "IS TO BE PARALLEL TO GRADE"; top of joint is depressed 1/8" below top of slab (pdf 210, section B-B).
- Section at curb (pdf 209): steel rail edge at 60 degrees, 6" height, continuous neoprene gland, edge of blockout; the horizontal
  dimension at the barrier face is "TO BE SET BY MANUFACTURER".
- Concrete in the blockout is placed after the joint is installed, after girder erection and deck placement on each side.
- Expansion device plans (one per joint; Pier 19G on pdf 209; Pier 6A (5WB and East Abutment "similar"), Pier 11WB, West Abutment on
  pdf 210): show the skewed joint line across the deck, support boxes (dashed), anchor bolts at regular spacing (not dimensioned),
  barrier rail ends, 1 1/2" recess in barrier side for slider plate travel, gutter line, and slider plate lengths. Barrier-end
  dimensions printed: 4'-0 overall with 2'-7 and 1'-6 (Pier 6A sketch); 5'-0 overall with 2'-9 / 2'-2 (Pier 11WB); 4'-3 overall with 2'-0
  / 2'-0 and 2'-7 (Pier 19G); 4'-10 overall with 3'-8 and 1'-2 (West Abutment). UNCERTAIN: which segment each number spans (skewed plan
  and overlapping extension lines); re-read before using.
- Barrier cover (slider) plates (pdf 210, design sheet 203): 3/8" A36 plates, 3/4" bent bolts 8" long, 3/4" x 1" countersunk cap screws,
  recess 1 1/2" in barrier sides and 3/4" at the barrier top, plate depressed 1/4" below barrier face. Barrier profile at the joint
  (section B-B) is fully dimensioned (2'-10 high; 8 1/2, 3 1/2, 5 widths at the gutter side; neutral-cure silicone caulk at the plate).

### 2.3 Left to the manufacturer
Quoted briefly from pdf 209 notes:

- "ANCHORAGE FOR MODULAR EXPANSION JOINT AND SPACING OF SUPPORT BRACKETS" are designed by the manufacturer.
- Gap between edge beams ("**"), number and size of beams, support bars and boxes, anchor spacing, the curb-side set dimension.
- "BLOCKOUT DETAILS MAY BE ALTERED FROM THOSE SHOWN" provided the gland can be installed and removed and the curb stays watertight.
- Material: A-36 minimum, galvanized after welding; rail pieces 15 ft minimum; centre beam splices full penetration.
- Number of seals: not stated. Only total movement per location is given. No approved-product list for the modular joints.
- Pay items (pdf 3): 2499-9000000 MODULAR EXPANSION JOINT ASSEMBLY (LF, 405.4) and 2499-9000100 MOD EXP JOINT ASSEMBLY LEAK
  TESTING (6 each). General notes (pdf 5) invoke the "DEVELOPMENTAL SPECIFICATIONS FOR MODULAR EXPANSION JOINT ASSEMBLY"; pdf 3 says
  the item includes anchorage, neoprene glands, temporary erection materials and barrier cover plates.

## 4. Verdict and minimum inputs for a modeler
(a) Fully modelable from the plans: none. Barrier cover plates, blockout outline and deck-end faces are dimensioned; the joint body is not.
(b) Envelope/blockout with a generic body: YES for the modular joints (all I-80 locations and Ramp G 5G, 9G, 14G, 19G) and YES for the
Ramp G south abutment strip seal (its anchorage plates can additionally be modeled).
Minimum inputs for (b):
1. Joint line: CL pier or CL abutment bearing, skew, and the CL of joint.
2. Deck-end faces: half gap each side of the CL joint (4 1/2" piers, 5" at Pier 14G, from CL pier; 6" to backwall at abutments; 2 1/2" strip seal),
   with CL bearing to deck end (table 2.4 / 3.2).
3. Blockout: D each side (25" piers, 18" abutments), depth 1'-2, following the skewed deck-end line across the deck width; flared
   4" +/- for the strip seal.
4. Generic body: total gap width, top 1/8" below top of slab, height within the 1'-2 depth, continuous along the joint (lin ft per pdf 4).
5. Barrier cover plates per 2.2; deck end block 2'-2 with the 1'-2 notch; end reinforcing marks.
(c) Not enough information: beam count and sizes, support bar/box sizes and spacing, anchors, gland product and number of seals.

## 6. Confirmed (raster checks, 2026-10-10)
PDF page numbers (1-based) of `B00168` (I-80, design 116) and `B00169` (Ramp G, design 316).
- **C and D (I-80 pdf 209, section at pier; pdf 185 section C-C):** C = opening between the two deck-end faces at 50 F (the "DIM. C *" arrows span
  the slab ends; 9" = 2 x 4 1/2" from CL pier, abutments 6" to the backwall face). D = blockout width from the deck-end face to the blockout edge,
  perpendicular to the joint: pdf 185 dimension string "2'-1" runs from the blockout edge to the deck end (1'-1 1/2 CL bearing to deck end,
  4 1/2" @ 50 F to CL pier). 25" at piers, 18" at abutments (table). Section depth 1'-2, "CL expansion joint" at the middle.
- **Ramp G Pier 14G:** C = 10" (5" each side), CL bearing to deck end 1'-1 (pdf 137-138 and table pdf 152).
- **Pier 19G (Ramp G side), Ramp G pdf 140 (design 138, Unit 4 slab):** CL bearing to deck end 1'-3 1/2, CL bearing to CL pier 1'-8, 4 1/2" @ 50 F,
  blockout edge to deck end 2'-1. The 19G joint is the Ramp G Unit 4 end; its other side (I-80 Unit 3 north part, "Pier 11WB & 19G") has no
  separate support line in the model.
- **Joint line:** not given by the joint sheets; the deck-end face is dimensioned normal to the support line ("DESIGN FOR VARIABLE SKEW ON HORIZ. CURVES"
  on the joint sheets), so the joint line is parallel to the support line at the deck-end offset; skews come from the model's supports.
- **Pier 6A:** a separate line and a separate joint (own row in the movement table and in the quantities, pdf 4 and 209) beside Pier 5WB, serving
  girders A-E; the model has one straight deck end per unit, so the 6A joint is NOT modeled separately (the 5WB joint line crosses the whole deck).
- **Strip seal (Ramp G pdf 150, section C-C and expansion opening detail):** "D" is the JOINT SETTING (2" at 50 F, 15/16" at 90 F, 3 1/16" at 10 F);
  concrete ends are D + 1/2" apart = 2 1/2" at 50 F. The pocket is "4 +/-" each side of the gland (flared); its depth is not dimensioned (model: 4" wide,
  8 1/16" = slab end block depth, approximate).
- **Model conflicts found by joints-i80.csx:** Pier 11WB support lines are 3'-2 apart in the model, the plans give 2 x 1'-8 = 3'-4 (opening 7" instead of 9");
  abutment backwall front face is 17" from CL bearing in the model, 22" per plan (deck end 1'-4 + C 6"). Not changed.

## 7. What the model builds
`SupportDefinition.ExpansionJoint` (`ExpansionJointDefinition`, optional, default off) on an END support: blockout (width D from the deck-end face,
full deck width, depth min(1'-2, slab)) is cut out of the deck and made as its own solid ("Joint blockout concrete"); a generic device envelope
(opening C wide, set 1/8" down, to blockout depth, between the barrier faces) is the placeholder "Generic modular expansion joint (manufacturer-designed
placeholder)". The plans' 2'-2 end block (deeper than the modeled 8" slab) is OPTIONAL (`ExpansionJointDefinition.EndBlock`, see `deck-end-blocks.md`); without it a warning says the blockout is limited to the slab. Sample data: `samples/plans/joints-i80.csx`, `joints-ramp-g.csx`.
Not modeled: barrier slider/cover plates, beams, support boxes, anchors, seals, drainage troughs.


## Abutment backwall front (22" vs 17") - resolved in the as-designed abutment
See `abutments-model.md`: the as-designed abutment models the plan 22" (I-80) / 17" (Ramp G) from the CL bearing; deck end (1'-4 / 1'-2 1/2) to backwall face is the plan 6" / 2 1/2" gap, so the joint line and deck end are unchanged.
