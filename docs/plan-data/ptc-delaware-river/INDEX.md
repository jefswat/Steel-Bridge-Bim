Richardson, Consulting Engineer, Pittsburgh PA; Associates Moran, Proctor, Mueser & Rutledge (consulting engineers, New York) and Harbeson, Hough, Livingston & Larson (architects, Philadelphia). Drawing numbers = the DRB series, one per contract: DRB-1 = contract PN 1 (river piers 17-20), DRB-2 = PN 2 (Pennsylvania approach substructure), DRB-3 = PN 3 (New Jersey approach substructure), DRB-4 = PN 4 (all steel superstructure, 68 sheets), DRB-10 = reference borings. "DRB-4/29" = DRB-4 sheet 29 of 68. For DRB-4, PDF page = sheet + 40. Dates are the title block drawing dates (revision dates, mostly 1954-57 "As Built" revisions, are on the sheets). Drawn / traced / checked initials are in each block and not tabulated.
