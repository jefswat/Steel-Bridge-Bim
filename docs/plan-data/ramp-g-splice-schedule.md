# Ramp G (Design 316) field splice schedule

Source: PDF page 106 = design sheet 104 (FIELD SPLICE DETAILS; title-block sheet number 105). Girder elevations (splice locations, A vs B-D): PDF 92-102. Transcribed from 200 dpi crops; fractions as a/b; sizes are thickness x width x length in inches (lengths ft-in).

## How to read it
- Schedule is per UNIT (4 tables). Units: 1 = FS1-8, 2 = FS9-14, 3 = FS15-22, 4 = FS23-30. In units 1-3 the suffix A = girder A, B = girders B, C and D (elevation sheets: 'Girder A elevation' / 'Girder B, C & D elevation'). Unit 4 splices (23-30) have NO suffix = all girders A-D (rows below tagged A-D). The schedule groups splice numbers per row; this file expands each to its own row (52 rows).
- Plates: B = top outer (1), C = top inner (2 required), A = web (2 required), D = bottom inner (2 required), E = bottom outer (1). 'FILL PL AS REQUIRED' under the outer and inner plates at both flanges (no thicknesses given). Gap at joint 1/4 in (typ.).
- MIN. GIRDER FLANGE WIDTH selects the bolt pattern (18 in = the '18 in min' detail, 20 to 34 = the '20 in to 34 in min' detail). The schedule lists only that minimum width; actual flange widths/thicknesses each side are on the girder elevation sheets, not in this schedule.
- Bolt spacing (1) = N spaces at the pitch on EACH SIDE of the joint CL. First bolt is 2 in from the splice CL (the '2' dimension at CL) and plate end = 1 1/2 in past the last bolt (check: 2'-11 = 35 in = 2 x (2 + 14 + 1.5)). Web columns likewise start 2 in from CL (3 = spacing, 1 @ 3 gives columns at 2 and 5 in each side; 2 @ 3 gives 2, 5, 8) with 1 1/2 in edge (13 in plate = 2 x 6.5, 19 in plate = 2 x 9.5).
- Web: row spacing (4) = row spaces from the top row to the bottom row; Dim S = top of web to the top bolt row; 1 1/2 in edge above and below (plate length = row span + 3 in, e.g. 21 @ 3 1/2 = 73.5 in, plate 6'-4 1/2 = 76.5 in).
- Flange bolt lines, 18 in detail: 4 lines, innermost at 2 1/2 in each side of CL, outer lines at gage (5) further = 7 1/2 in, edge distance 1 1/2 in. STAGGERED: along each side the bolts alternate between outer and inner line at the bolt pitch (the bolt nearest the joint is on an OUTER line; so 'N spaces' = N+1 bolts per side split between outer/inner). Outer plate has chamfered (clipped) corners at both plate ends in the detail (no dimension given). Narrower flange tapers 1:2.5 to the wider at the joint ('2 (TYP.)').
- Flange bolt lines, 20 to 34 in detail: NOT staggered (aligned columns on all lines). Innermost line 3 in from CL (3 + 3), 2 in edge distance, line pitch = gage: 20 w/ gage 5 -> 3, 8; 22 w/ 6 -> 3, 9; 24 w/ 2 @ 3 1/2 -> 3, 6.5, 10; 26 w/ 2 @ 4 -> 3, 7, 11; 34 w/ 4 @ 3 -> 3, 6, 9, 12, 15 (all verified against edge distance 2). Square plate ends, 1 1/2 in end distance, no clips drawn.
- Note: '1 1/2' above/below the web rows and 1 1/2 beside the web columns on the web plate are edge distances.
