## Common (all three)
- Superstructure Unit 4 (spans 167'-0, 198'-0, 258'-0, 205'-0, 152'-0); piers 15G..19G (p11, p17). Four girders A-D.
- Piles HP14x73 (all three), contract length 70 FEET each (p67, p71, p74); min embedment 50 ft (16G, 17G) / 55 ft (18G)
  (p67, p71, p74). "Pile dimensions are at bottom of footing"; pile top is 1'-0 above the footing bottom (p64-66 footing
  elevation "1'-0 / TOP OF PILE / 1" CL.").
- Steel pile points required; uplift-anchor detail (design sheet 19) at circled piles (p66, p70, p73). 8f6 = 5'-0 uplift anchors.
- Mechanical splices at 16G, 17G, 18G (p3); temporary shoring required at 16G, 17G, 18G (p3). Work along the pier line for 16G
  must be done first (railroad realignment adjacent, p6).
- Bearings: disc. Anchor bolts: 16G/17G 1 1/2"dia headed (girders A, D) and swedge (girders B, C); 18G 2 1/2"dia swedge.
  Bolt pattern per girder: 9" each side of cap CL (18" across) x 1'-10 each side of girder CL (3'-8 along girder) (p65, p69, p72).
- Notes: "ELEVATION AND PEDESTAL HEIGHTS DEPENDENT ON FINAL BEARING HEIGHT" - * marks pedestal-top elevations (p64, p68, p72).
