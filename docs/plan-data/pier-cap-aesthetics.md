# Pier cap aesthetic treatment (Design 116 I-80 WB, Design 316 Ramp G)

Transcribed from the PIER AESTHETIC DETAILS sheets; dimensions and tables checked on 200-250 dpi raster clips
(scripts/PNGs in `temp/extract/aesthetics/`). "PDF n" = PDF page, "DS n" = design sheet number.

Sources
- I-80 WB (`B00168 ... Design 116.pdf`): PDF 83 (DS 81) notes + pier 6A figure + Details A/B; PDF 84 (DS 82) generic piers 1WB-15WB,
  table, pier 11WB figure, wash and plinth details. General notes PDF 6 (DS 4).
- Ramp G (`B00169 ... Design 316.pdf`): PDF 78 (DS 76, revised 12-01-15) notes + pier 19G + piers 1G-15G & 18G + details;
  PDF 79 (DS 77) piers 16G, 17G table. General notes PDF 6 (DS 4).
- Both sets use the same details. All views are "looking ahead station".

## 1. What the treatment is

A FORM-LINER TEXTURE on the cap face, bounded below by a curved (reverse-curve) line, plus a small stepped fan at one cap end.
It is not a deep recess; the relief is the texture depth and the 3/4" steps.

- Texture "B": vertical fractured-face ribs, approx. 2" centres, max depth 1 1/2", min depth 1 3/8" (PDF 83, PDF 78 text;
  the min depth on Ramp G is inside a revision cloud "REVISED DEPTH OF PIER CONCRETE TEXTURE").
- Texture "A": ashlar stone masonry, max depth 1 1/2" (Ramp G value is under a revision cloud; I-80 reads 1 1/2). Used only on the
  ARCHITECTURAL PLINTH at the column bases (PDF 84 PIER PLINTH DETAIL, PDF 78 same), not on the cap. Plinth: 1" wash at the top
  (WASH DETAIL), height per the individual pier elevations, 1'-0 MIN. below proposed groundline. Columns themselves untextured.
- Cap surfaces (PDF 83, 84, 78, 79):
  - Face between the cap top and the curved lower boundary (hatched vertical) = TEXTURE "B".
  - Cap end faces (raked, 75 deg on 6A, PDF 83) = TEXTURE "B".
  - The other face: I-80 PDF 83 pier 6A: "NO TEXTURE (FAR FACE) / TEXTURE B (NEAR FACE)"; PDF 84 pier 11WB: "NO TEXTURE (NEAR FACE) /
    TEXTURE B (FAR FACE)"; Ramp G PDF 78 pier 19G: "NO TEXTURE (NEAR FACE) / TEXTURE B (FAR FACE)". The sheets are not
    consistent between piers, so the textured face is per pier (UNCERTAIN, see end).
  - Region below the curved line and the soffit: "NO TEXTURE". "TEXTURE B (UNDERSIDE OF STEP) (TYP.)" = the underside of each
    step also gets texture B.
- Curved line: S-shaped, drawn as tangent circular arcs with "POINT OF REVERSE CURVATURE" and "CONTROL POINT (TYP.)" marks.
  At the end where the cap tapers (left end in the 6A and 19G figures; right end in the generic 1WB-15WB figure, mirrored on
  Ramp G 1G-15G) three nested arcs fan out (R3, R4, R5 or the third radius set) with "2 @ 3/4" = 1 1/2"" annotated between them
  and a spacing "L4" along the cap: two steps of 3/4" each (total 1 1/2").
- DETAIL A (PDF 83, PDF 78; circled at the cap tip, "SEE DETAIL A (TYP.)"): plan-type sketch of the tip: "1/2" SETBACK (TYP.)",
  3" and 3" from the work point W.P., Texture B on one side, No Texture on the other, "CORNERS SHALL NOT BE CHAMFERED".
- DETAIL B (same sheets): section through a setback: "NO CHAMFER", "7 deg 0'0" (MIN.)"; note "APPLY 7 DEGREE (MIN.) DRAFT AT ALL
  SETBACKS."
- Rustication: "THE PIER SURFACES AS DESIGNATED IN THE PLANS SHALL ALSO RECEIVE CONCRETE RUSTICATION" (PDF 83, PDF 78); the
  rustication notes on PDF 6 are only materials/methods. No groove width/depth/section is dimensioned anywhere on the pier
  aesthetic sheets.

## 3. Reinforcing / cover implications
- Cap elevations are titled "PIER CAP ELEVATION (SHOWING REINFORCING IN ARCHITECTURAL RELIEF)" (e.g. Pier 1G PDF 20, Pier 2WB-15WB
  cap elevation sheets). The elevation shows face bars (5a9-5a13 "E.F.") following the curved lower edge. No cover value or
  bent-around-relief note is given for the relief; the only stated clearance is 2" CL. on column sections.
- Mockup note: one mat of #5 bars each way, 2" clear to the bottom of the textured face (PDF 83).

## 4. Finish
Form-liner patterns only (Texture A: CustomRock 12020, Fitzgerald 17000, Architectural Polymers 905, Spec Formliners 1515;
Texture B: Architectural Polymers 204, Fitzgerald 16959, Greenstreak 367, Scott System 129A, Custom Rock 206). No color, stain
or sealer is specified for the pier caps on these sheets (colored sealer is mentioned only for patching mortar, PDF 6).

## 5. Modeling proposal
Everything is in the pier plane (S, elevation) with a depth normal to the cap face (up/down station), so it fits the cap polygon:
1. `Aesthetic.Face` = UpStation | DownStation (per pier; see UNCERTAIN 1).
2. `Aesthetic.Band` = polygon in (S, depth-below-top) built from L1, L2, L3, band depth (3'-6 at the control points; the end depth is
   the cap end depth), R1, R2 (tangent arcs through the control points), R3/R4/R5, L4. Use the table values as per-pier data.
3. `Aesthetic.StepDepth` = 3/4" (2 steps -> 1 1/2"), `CornerSetback` = 1/2", `TextureDepth` = 1 1/2" max.
One parametric rule (the L/R set) covers piers 1WB-15WB, 16G/17G and 1G-15G/18G with a small table; 6A and 19G need explicit
points (their figures carry their own dimensions). A minimal first implementation: a 1 1/2" (or 3/4") recess polygon on the
textured face along the lower arc plus the end-fan steps; texture itself need not be modeled.

## CONFIRMED (re-read on 220-400 dpi raster clips, PDF 78, 79, 83, 84; cap step from `docs/plan-data/piles-capstep-dowels.md` item 2)

1. TEXTURED FACE. Only the figure notes say: PDF 83 pier 6A "NO TEXTURE (FAR FACE) / TEXTURE B (NEAR FACE)"; PDF 84 pier 11WB and PDF 78 pier 19G
   "NO TEXTURE (NEAR FACE) / TEXTURE B (FAR FACE)". All views are "looking ahead station": near = back (down-station, N0), far = ahead (up-station, N1).
   The generic figures (1WB-15WB, 1G-15G/18G, 16G/17G) carry no near/far note: modeled as the far (ahead) face like 11WB / 19G (UNRESOLVABLE from the
   sheets; flagged). Texture is not geometry (it is only the face tag `CapAestheticsDefinition.Face`); the cap SECTION STEP is on both faces.
2. WHAT THE STEPS ARE. Sections A-A/B-B (item 2 of the piles/capstep doc): cap full width W above the relief curve, W - 1'-0 below it (6" setback per face,
   both faces), as one 6" step or three 2" steps through the fan end. The three nested fan arcs R3 > R4 > R5 ARE the three step lines (they start together
   at the point of reverse curvature and fan out; "2 @ 3/4 = 1 1/2" is the spacing mark at the tip, not a step height). So: layers = [R5, R4, R3] (lowest
   line first), 2" thick each, stacked outward from the lower section; tread drafted 7 deg (Detail B), no chamfers. Detail B orientation: the upper
   (textured) part projects, its underside slopes up toward the outside; the sketch cannot say more. Detail A (1/2" setback at the beak tip, 3" / 3" from the
   W.P.): NOT modeled (plan-detail sketch only; flagged). The textured end faces are not modeled either.
3. L1 and "VARIES". L1 = top corner to top corner of the cap (matches the structural cap: 2 x half length for every I-80 pier, 63'/53' on 16G/17G).
   The dimension strings of the explicit figures run from the left top corner. 1G-15G/18G: cap 35'-0, the point of reverse curvature (CL pier) is 17'-6 from
   each corner, so both "VARIES" end lengths are 17'-6 - 9'-6 = 8'-0 (the 1'-9 / 1'-9 / 6'-0 break marks are the fan control points 9'-6 / 7'-9 / 6'-0 from the CL).
   CONFLICT 19G: the aesthetic sheet puts the CL-pier mark 18'-0 1/2 from the left corner (8'-3 1/2 + 9'-9) and 22'-0 from the right (7'-2 + 1'-11 + 1'-11 +
   11'-0 1/16); `piers-ramp-g.csx` has the corners at -21.0 / +19.0 from the column. The aesthetic chain is used as printed (it adds up to the 40'-0 cap).
   CONFLICT beak depth: the sheets give the band depth at the long-side tip as 6'-9 (6A) and 6'-6 1/4 (19G, 1G-15G, 18G), the structural pier sheets give the beak
   tip 5'-0 (6A) / 5'-6 below the top; the modeled caps keep the structural tips, so the long arc enters the cap through the soffit bevel a little inside the tip.
4. TEXTURE DEPTHS under the revision cloud (PDF 78, 12-01-15): Texture A max 1 1/2"; Texture B max 1 1/2", min 1 3/8" (the I-80 sheet prints the same).
   No effect on the geometry (the setback is 6").

How the curve is built (code: `CapBandBuilder`): arcs are tangent; depths are measured perpendicular to the top line, stations along it from the left corner.
- Table piers (I-80 1WB-15WB, 16G, 17G): hill arc R1 -> reverse point P at L-hill, 3'-6 deep -> sag arc R2 -> reverse point Q at L-fan from the other end, 3'-6 deep ->
  three hill arcs R3/R4/R5 tangent at Q. 16G/17G are the mirror image with L2 on the fan side. The arcs end where they meet the cap outline (end face / soffit bevel).
  Check against the sheets: L4 (spacing of the arc ends) comes out 1.5-1.7 ft smaller than printed (e.g. 4WB 1.7 vs 2.25) -- L4 is not used.
- Explicit figures (6A, 19G, 1G-15G, 18G): the long arc (R84 / R60 / R40, sag) from the tip depth to the labeled point of reverse curvature, then the three fan arcs
  tangent from it. The control-point depths printed on the sheets are reproduced to: 6A -8/-5/-1 in, 19G +5/+8/+12 in, 1G +0/+1/+5 in (model - sheet): the printed
  figures are schematic and over-determined (UNRESOLVED; the residuals are printed by `pier-aesthetics.csx`).
